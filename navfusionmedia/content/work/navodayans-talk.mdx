## The idea

Students in Navodaya schools rarely hear from people who sat in the same classrooms and hostels. Navodayans Talk fixes that. Each episode is one long conversation with a JNV alumnus about how they got where they are.

## What we do

- **Find guests.** We reach out to alumni through school networks and referrals.
- **Prepare.** We research each guest and write questions around their story, not their CV.
- **Record.** Most episodes are recorded remotely. We guide guests on camera, light and sound.
- **Edit.** We cut the long episode, clean the audio and add Hindi captions.
- **Design.** Every episode gets a thumbnail with the guest's photo and a short Hindi hook.
- **Clip.** We pull 3–5 short clips per episode for Reels and Shorts.

## What we learned

- Short Hindi hooks on thumbnails get more clicks than long English titles.
- Guests talk more freely when the first 10 minutes are about school, not career.
- Clips with a clear question in the first second hold viewers longer.
