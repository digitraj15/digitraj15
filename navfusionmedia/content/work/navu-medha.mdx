## The problem

Many JNVST prep books are dense, hard to read, and written for adults, not 10-year-olds. Students in small towns often study without a teacher to explain things.

## What we made

- **Short chapters.** One idea per chapter, explained in simple Hindi.
- **Stories.** Each chapter opens with a short story set in a village, so concepts feel familiar.
- **Practice.** Multiple-choice questions after each chapter, with an answer key.
- **Clean layout.** Large type, clear diagrams and plenty of space to work.

## Sections covered

- Mental ability
- Arithmetic
- Language
